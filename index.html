How tailoring uses this

  1. Paste your current resume. It is the source of truth: Claude rewrites and reorders it for each job but is told not to invent jobs, degrees or numbers.
  2. Skills drive the match score on every listing.
  3. Hobbies and interests flag jobs that connect to them, and can go into a short “Interests” section when they help.
  4. Your home city is the “Home” location in the filters.

Claude API key

Outside the Claude app, tailoring uses your own key from console.anthropic.com. It is kept in this browser only and sent straight to Anthropic.
